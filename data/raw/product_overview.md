# Nimbus Cloud Storage — Product Overview

Nimbus is a cloud object storage service for teams that need secure, versioned file storage
with fine-grained access control.

## Plans

- **Starter**: 100 GB storage, 5 team members, 30-day version history. Price: $9/month.
- **Team**: 1 TB storage, 25 team members, 90-day version history. Price: $49/month.
- **Enterprise**: Unlimited storage, unlimited members, 1-year version history, SSO/SAML,
  dedicated support. Custom pricing — contact sales.

## Key Features

- Client-side encryption before upload (AES-256).
- Real-time collaborative folders with conflict-free sync.
- Granular sharing: per-file and per-folder permissions (viewer, commenter, editor, owner).
- Audit log of every access, download, and permission change, retained for 1 year on
  Enterprise plans and 90 days on Team plans.
- API access on Team and Enterprise plans, rate-limited to 1000 requests/minute.

## Data Residency

Enterprise customers can pin their storage region to US, EU, or APAC data centers. Starter
and Team plans store data in the US region only.
