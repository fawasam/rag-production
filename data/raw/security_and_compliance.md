# Nimbus Security & Compliance

## Encryption

All files are encrypted client-side with AES-256 before leaving the user's device, and
again at rest on Nimbus servers. Data in transit is protected with TLS 1.3.

## Compliance Certifications

Nimbus holds SOC 2 Type II certification, renewed annually. Enterprise plans additionally
support HIPAA-compliant configurations via a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA),
available on request from the sales team.

## Access Control

Enterprise plans support SSO via SAML 2.0 and SCIM-based user provisioning. Team and
Starter plans use email/password or Google/Microsoft OAuth login only; SSO is not
available below the Enterprise tier.

## Incident Response

In the event of a security incident affecting customer data, Nimbus commits to notifying
affected Enterprise customers within 72 hours, per the terms of the Enterprise SLA.
Team and Starter customers are notified via the status page and email within 5 business
days.

## Data Deletion

When an account is closed, all customer data is permanently deleted from primary storage
within 30 days and from backups within 90 days.
