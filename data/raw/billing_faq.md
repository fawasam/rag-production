# Nimbus Billing FAQ

## How does billing work?

Nimbus bills monthly in advance, on the calendar date you first subscribed. Annual billing
is available at a 15% discount, charged once per year on the anniversary date.

## What happens if I exceed my storage limit?

On the Starter and Team plans, if you exceed your storage limit, uploads are blocked but
existing files remain accessible. You'll receive an email warning at 90% usage. Enterprise
plans have no storage limit, so this does not apply.

## Can I change plans mid-cycle?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and you're charged a prorated difference for the
remainder of the billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing
cycle; you keep your current plan's features until then.

## What is the refund policy?

Nimbus offers a full refund within 14 days of the initial charge for any plan. After 14
days, charges are non-refundable, but you may cancel at any time to stop future billing.

## Do you offer non-profit or education discounts?

Yes, verified non-profit and education accounts receive 30% off the Team plan. Enterprise
discounts are negotiated individually with sales.
